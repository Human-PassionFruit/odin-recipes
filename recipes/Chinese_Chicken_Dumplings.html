Chinese Chicken Dumplings

Description

Chinese Chicken Dumplings are a popular chinese dish that is very similar to the popular siomai which is one of Filipino's favorite street food items in the philippines.

Difficulty

level: Advanced

Prep time: 30 minutes

Cook time: 20 minutes

Total time: 50 minutes

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
ground chicken 1 pound
green onions, chopped 4 pcs
chopped cabbage 1/2 cup
minced fresh cilantro 1/4 cup
minced fresh gingerroot 2 teaspoons
Chinese five-spice powder 1/4 teaspoon
salt 1 teaspoon
water 2 tablespoons
Cabbage leavess any amount
Reduced-sodium soy sauce 1/4 cup
pot sticker or gyoza wrappers 1 package (10 ounces)

Steps

  1. Step 1: Prepare the Filling

    -Place the first 7 ingredients in a food processor. Process until finely chopped. Add water and blend again until well combined.

  2. Step 2: Fill and Fold the Dumplings

    -Place 1 tablespoon of chicken mixture in the center of a wrapper. Keep the remaining wrappers covered with a damp paper towel. Moisten the wrapper's edges with water, fold it in half over the filling, and press firmly to seal. Make 3–5 small folds along the front edge.

  3. Step 3: Shape the Dumplings

    -Hold the sealed edges upright and gently press the bottom of each dumpling against a flat surface so it can stand. Repeat with the remaining wrappers and filling. Cover the prepared dumplings with plastic wrap.

  4. Step 4: Steam the Dumplings

    -Line a steamer basket with cabbage leaves. Arrange the dumplings about 1 inch apart. Place the basket in a large saucepan with 1 inch of water. Bring the water to a boil, cover, and steam for 10–12 minutes or until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C). Repeat in batches as needed. Serve with soy sauce.

  5. Step 5: Serve

    -Serve the dumplings hot with soy sauce or your favorite dipping sauce.
