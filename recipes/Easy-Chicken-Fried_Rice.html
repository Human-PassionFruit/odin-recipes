Easy Chicken Fried Rice

Description

Easy Chicken Fried Rice is a quick and delicious one-pan meal that's perfect for anyone with natural cravings.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
frozen mixed vegetables 12 ounces
olive oil 2 tablespoons
eggs, lightly beaten 2 large
sesame oil 4 tablespoons
ready-to-serve garden vegetable rice 3 packages (8.8 ounces each)
rotisserie chicken 1 whole chicken, cut into pieces, skin removed
salt 1/4 teaspoon
pepper 1/4 teaspoon

Steps

  1. Step 1: Prepare the Eggs and Vegetables

    -Cook the frozen vegetables according to the package instructions. Meanwhile, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the eggs and stir until fully cooked. Remove the eggs from the skillet and set aside.

  2. Step 2: Cook the Rice

    -In the same skillet, heat 2 tablespoons of sesame oil and the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil. Add the rice and stir-fry for 10–12 minutes, or until it begins to brown.

  3. Step 3: Combine the Ingredients

    -Add the chicken, salt, and pepper to the rice. Stir in the cooked eggs and vegetables. Mix everything together until heated through, breaking the eggs into smaller pieces. Finally, drizzle with the remaining 2 tablespoons of sesame oil and serve.

  4. Step 4: Serve and Enjoy

    -Serve the Easy Chicken Fried Rice hot and enjoy your meal!
