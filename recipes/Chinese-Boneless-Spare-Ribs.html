Chinese Boneless Spare Ribs

Description

Chinese Boneless Spare Ribs is a delicious and easy-to-make dish that's good for any meal.

Difficulty

level: Easy

Prep time: 15 minutes

Cook time: 20 minutes

Total time: 35 minutes

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Soy sauce 1/2 cup
Hoisin sauce 1/2 cup
Dry red wine 1/4 cup
Sugar 4 tablespoons
Garlic(minced) 2 cloves
Five-spice powder/cinnamon powder 1/2 teaspoon
Boneless Pork shoulder/loin 1-1/2 pounds

Steps

  1. Step 1: Prepare the Marinade

    -In a small bowl, mix the soy sauce, hoisin sauce, red wine, sugar, minced garlic, and Chinese five-spice powder. Add red food coloring if desired. Set aside ¼ cup of the marinade in a covered container and refrigerate.

  2. Step 2: Marinate the Pork

    -Place the pork strips in a large shallow dish. Pour the remaining marinade over the pork, making sure all pieces are coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight..

  3. Step 3: Bake the Pork

    -Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Drain the pork and discard the used marinade. Arrange the pork on a greased wire rack over a foil-lined baking sheet. Bake for 30 minutes, turning the pieces halfway through. Brush with the reserved marinade.

  4. Step 4: Broil the Pork

    -Place the baking sheet 4–6 inches from the broiler. Broil for 2–3 minutes or until the edges are lightly browned. Watch carefully to prevent burning.

  5. Step 5: Check and serve.

    -Make sure the pork is fully cooked. For safety, pork should reach an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C), followed by a 3-minute rest. Slice if needed and serve.
